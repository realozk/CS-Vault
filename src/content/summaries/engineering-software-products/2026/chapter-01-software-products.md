# Chapter 1: Software Products - Detailed Summary

## Software Products vs. Custom Software (Slides 2-8)
*   **Software products** are generic, designed for a broad customer base, and their development starts with identifying a business **opportunity**. The development company decides on features and timescales (rapid delivery is crucial).
*   **Custom software systems** are developed for a specific client to solve a specific **problem** based on client **requirements**. The customer can change requirements at any time, and the software has a long lifespan.

## Technologies and Execution Models (Slides 9-12)
*   **Software Product Line:** A set of products sharing a common core but adapted for specific customers.
*   **Platform:** A software/hardware product containing functionality that allows new applications to be built upon it (e.g., Facebook).
*   **Execution Models:**
    *   *Stand-alone:* Executes entirely on the customer's computer.
    *   *Hybrid:* Functionality is split between the user's computer and the developer's servers.
    *   *Software as a Service (SaaS):* All features are on the developer's servers; accessed via browser/app.

## Product Vision and Information Sources (Slides 14-18)
*   **Product Vision:** A simple statement answering: What is the product? Who are the customers? Why buy it? 
*   **Moore's Vision Template:** FOR (target customer) WHO (need), The (PRODUCT NAME) is a (category) THAT (key benefit) UNLIKE (competitor) OUR PRODUCT (differentiation).
*   **Information Sources:** Domain experience, Product experience, Customer experience, and Prototyping ("playing around").

## Software Product Management & Prototyping (Slides 19-30)
*   **Product Managers (PMs):** Bridge the gap between the business, the development team, and the customers.
*   **PM Concerns:** Business needs, technology constraints, and customer experience.
*   **PM Technical Interactions:** Managing product vision, product roadmap development, user story/scenario creation, product backlog management, acceptance testing, customer testing, and UI design.
*   **Prototyping:** Crucial first step to demonstrate features. Always plan to throw it away and rebuild.
    *   *Feasibility demonstration:* Proves ideas work and shows superiority over competitors.
    *   *Customer demonstration:* Extends the feasibility prototype with specific customer features based on user studies.
